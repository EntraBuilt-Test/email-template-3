A clean, professional, and authoritative email template created for **Nordic Affairs Media** (`nordicaffairsmedia.com`, Stockholm, Sweden). Designed with a warm yellow brand header (`#FCC66C`), crisp white body, dual hero ceremony photos, the company's 3 core institutional pillars, and a **single prominent Enquiry button**.

## 🌟 Template Highlights & Content

1. **Header Banner**:
   - Signature warm yellow `#FCC66C` header bar displaying **NORDIC AFFAIRS MEDIA** and *"Stockholm, Sweden • Swedish Press Association Accredited"*.
2. **Top Hero Ceremony Banner**:
   - `Royal_ceremony_in_palace_hall_657f1abc_2K.jpg` showcasing premier royal ceremony coverage.
3. **Introduction**:
   - *"Documenting Historic Moments with Excellence & Authenticity"*.
   - Simple description of trusted coverage for diplomatic visits, royal ceremonies, and government engagements.
4. **3 Core Institutional Pillars (from `nordicaffairsmedia.com/en/about-us`)**:
   - 🎖️ **Professional Standards**: Officially accredited by the Swedish Press Association. Delivering accuracy, discretion, and world-class visual storytelling.
   - 👁️ **Our Vision**: Creating impactful visual stories that preserve important global moments, showcase diplomacy, leadership, and culture.
   - 🌍 **Based in Sweden — Working Worldwide**: Headquartered in Stockholm, delivering premium event coverage across Sweden and international destinations.
5. **Bottom Hero Ceremony Banner**:
   - `Royal_ceremony_in_palace_courtyard_91dd2e77_2K.jpg` featuring royal courtyard ceremony coverage.
6. **The Only Button — Prominent Enquiry**:
   - Exactly **one single button** in the entire email: **`ENQUIRY →`** in high-contrast golden yellow (`#FFD24C` with `#111827` bold text).
   - Links directly to `https://nordicaffairsmedia.com/en`.
7. **Clean Footer**:
   - Stockholm headquarters, Swedish Press Association accreditation, website link, and unsubscribe.
